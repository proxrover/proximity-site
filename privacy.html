Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This policy describes how Proximity Software Factory LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with this website and the apps we publish on the Apple App Store. We keep it short because we collect very little.

This website

proximity.guru is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no scripts, fonts, or other resources from third parties. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record standard server logs such as IP address and requested page in order to operate the service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details. We do not receive or review those logs.

If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply and keep a record of the conversation. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Our apps

The following applies to apps published by Proximity Software Factory LLC unless an app's own App Store listing links to a more specific policy, in which case that policy applies to that app.

  • Account. Where an app offers sign-in, it uses Sign in with Apple. We receive the identifier Apple provides and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. We use these only to operate your account.
  • Content you create. Content you enter or generate in an app is stored on your device and, where the app offers sync or server-side features, on servers we operate in the United States. It is used to provide the app's features to you.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through in-app purchase. We do not receive your payment details. We receive confirmation from Apple that a purchase or subscription is active.
  • AI features. Some apps include features powered by AI models. Where an app lets you supply your own AI provider credentials, your requests go directly to that provider under its terms. Where we host the model, your requests are processed on infrastructure we operate and are not used to train models.
  • Diagnostics. If an app crashes, Apple may share anonymized crash reports with us when you have opted in to share diagnostics with developers in iOS settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share personal information with third parties for their marketing.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in our apps.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing support@proximity.guru from the address linked to your account. We complete deletion within 30 days.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes that affect an app will also be noted in that app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@proximity.guru.
