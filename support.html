Support

For apps published by Proximity Software Factory LLC

If something in one of our apps is not working, or you have a question about your account or subscription, email us. A person reads every message, and we aim to reply within two business days.

support@proximity.guru

What to include

The more of this you can send, the faster we can help:

  • The name of the app and, if you know it, the version shown in its settings screen.
  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • What you were doing when the problem happened, and what you expected instead.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visible on screen.

Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. Refunds are issued by Apple; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you are stuck, email us and we will point you in the right direction.

Deleting your account

Where an app offers accounts, you can delete yours from within the app's settings. You can also email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days. See our privacy policy for what we store.

Something else

For questions that are not about an app, such as project inquiries, use info@proximity.guru.
